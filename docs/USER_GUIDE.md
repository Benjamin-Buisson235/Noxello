# Noxello — User Guide

Welcome to Noxello. This guide covers the core workflows: boards, lists, cards, and collaboration.

## 1) Getting Started
- Open the app and create an account (Register).
- Log in to access your boards.
- Use the top‑level Boards page to create your first board.

## 2) Boards
- **Create**: Use the “New board” form on the Boards page.
- **Rename**: Use the Rename action on a board card.
- **Delete**: Use Delete on a board card (confirmation required).
- **Shared boards**: Boards shared with you are marked as shared.

## 3) Lists (Columns)
Inside a board:
- **Add a list**: Use the “Add list” area.
- **Rename**: Click Rename on the list header.
- **Reorder**: Drag a list by its title.
- **Delete**: Delete list (confirmation required).

## 4) Cards
Inside a list:
- **Add a card**: Click “+ Add a card”, type a title, press Enter.
- **Open details**: Click a card.
- **Move between lists**: Drag & drop.
- **Reorder**: Drag & drop within the list.
- **Delete**: Use Delete (confirmation required).

## 5) Drag & Drop
- Drag cards within a list or across lists.
- Drag lists to reorder columns.
- The UI previews the target position.

## 6) Card Details
Open any card to edit:
- **Title & Description**
- **Due date** (custom picker)
- **Labels** (create / assign / delete)
- **Checklist** with progress
- **Comments** with author & timestamp
- **Archive / Unarchive**

### Due Date Picker
- Choose a date from the calendar.
- Click **Save** to apply.
- Use **Clear** to remove the due date.

## 7) Labels
- Create labels with a name and color.
- Assign labels to cards.
- Delete labels from the label manager.

## 8) Checklist
- Add checklist items.
- Toggle done / not done.
- Reorder with up/down.
- Delete items.

## 9) Comments
- Add a comment at the top of the comments section.
- Delete your own comments.

## 10) Archive
- Archive a card to remove it from normal view.
- View archived cards in the archived section.
- Unarchive to restore the card.

## 11) Search & Filters
- Search by title or description.
- Filter by due date and labels.

## 12) Members & Invites
- Owners can invite users by email.
- Members can view the board and collaborate.
- Owners can remove members.
- Invitations can be accepted or declined.
- Invites are available from the left sidebar on any page.

## 13) Settings
- Update your profile name in Settings.

## Troubleshooting
- **Board not loading**: refresh or log out/in.
- **Changes not visible**: refresh the board page.
- **CORS errors**: ensure backend `FRONTEND_ORIGIN` is set correctly.

## Tips
- Use drag & drop for quick reordering.
- Keep labels short for readability.
- Archive instead of deleting for safety.
